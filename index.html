(01) — About

I'm Vyacheslav — a mixing and mastering engineer working out of Odessa with artists all over the world.

Since 2018 I've put my hands on 265 released records across pop, hip-hop and metal, alongside house, latin and everything that refuses to sit in one folder. My job is to make a record translate — powerful and balanced on a club system, honest through phone speakers.

Certified for Apple Digital Masters, and ranked in the top 5% of mixing and mastering engineers on Muso.AI. Every project gets its own chain and its own decisions — no templates.
